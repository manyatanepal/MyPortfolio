Projects

Oasis Cafe

Oasis Cafe is a mobile cafe app designed to make ordering coffee and exploring the menu simple and enjoyable.
It includes features such as menu browsing, favorites, membership rewards and user profiles.

Clayistry

Clayistry is a mobile handmade pottery marketplace that connects pottery lovers with artists and their creations.
The app allows users to explore pottery, save favorites, discover artist stories and learn more about each piece.

Lunar Bakery

Lunar Bakery is a moon-inspired bakery branding concept based on the eight phases of the moon.
Each moon phase is represented through a unique pastry concept, creating a creative and memorable visual identity.
